FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Text us at (707) 555-0100 and a real person will get back to you.

Are quotes really free?

Yes. Quotes and site visits are free anywhere in our service area. Small jobs we can usually price from a quick description, bigger projects get a visit first.

What does a typical cleanup cost?

Most residential cleanups land between $250 and $450, depending on yard size, how overgrown it is and how much green waste we haul. You'll always get the exact price in writing before we start.

How soon can you come out?

Site visits are usually within the week. Cleanups and planting typically get scheduled within one to two weeks, a little longer in peak fall season.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. As long as we can get into the yard, we're good. Leave gate codes or notes about pets in your request and we'll text you when we're done.

Do you work in the rain?

This is Humboldt, so light rain doesn't stop us. If heavy rain would damage the lawn or beds, we'll text you to reschedule, no charge.

Can I set up recurring service?

Yes. Weekly or biweekly mowing, seasonal cleanups and year-round care plans. Skip or pause a visit anytime with a quick text.
